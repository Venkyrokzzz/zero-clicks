# Zero Clicks — YouTube Setup & Growth Plan

Authoritative copy lives in [[Marketing/YouTube Setup & Growth Plan.md]] (Obsidian vault). This file mirrors it for version control.

## Why YouTube

- YouTube is the second-largest search engine (owned by Google) — your videos appear in Google search too
- Pub/restaurant owners search: "how to reply to Google reviews", "Google Business Profile help", "restaurant marketing ideas"
- Video proves the product works — a 60-second demo of a drafted review reply beats text
- Long-term compound traffic: one optimized video brings views for months

## Content strategy — Hero / Hub / Help / Hype / Action

| Layer | Role | Format | Frequency |

| Hub | Binge-watching | Series, playlists | Weekly |

Funnel: Shorts attract → long video (demo/case study) → 0-clicks.uk → booking/trial

## Per-video SEO checklist

Before: pick ONE target keyword (YouTube autocomplete + vidIQ free), check top 5 ranking videos, make a better version, write title + thumbnail before shooting.
During: say keyword in first 30s, hook in first 10–15s, clear audio, cut fluff.
At upload: title keyword-first under 60–70 chars; description front-loads first 125 chars; custom thumbnail 1280×720 max 4–5 words; 5–10 tags; up to 3 hashtags; accurate captions; 3+ chapters starting 00:00; add to playlist; end screen + subscribe; location tag UK.
After: reply to early comments, check Studio at 24–48h and 7–28 days, adjust thumbnail/title if CTR low, rewrite description if impressions flat.

## KPIs

Impressions, CTR (>4–5%), average view duration (>50%), traffic source, subscribers, clicks to 0-clicks.uk.

## 30-day launch

Week 1: Channel setup + 2 Help videos + 3 Shorts. Week 2: 2 more videos + 5 Shorts. Week 3: 2 videos + 7 Shorts + end screens. Week 4: Review analytics, double down on best keyword, 10 Shorts, case-study outreach.

## Production (zero-to-low budget)

Camera: iPhone/Android. Audio: quiet pub corner or lapel mic. Lighting: natural window light, no backlight. Editing: CapCut / DaVinci Resolve / iMovie (free). Thumbnails: Canva free.
