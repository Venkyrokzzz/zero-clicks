# Zero Clicks — Content Calendar

30-day marketing calendar for acquiring the first paying clients.
Objective: first paying client at £55/month, then five founding clients at £35/month.
Zero budget, solo founder, ~4 hours/day.

Source: [[Business/Marketing Plan - Hermes Agent]] in the Obsidian vault.

## Stopping condition

200 targeted emails/DMs sent before evaluating whether messaging works. Until 200, volume is the only metric.

## Weekly targets

| Week | Cold emails (5/day) | Instagram DMs (20/day) | LinkedIn posts (3/wk) | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 25 | ~20 (selective) | Tue/Thu/Sat | Morning Advertiser pitch; collect before/after screenshots |
| Week 2 | 50 | ~20 | Tue/Thu/Sat | Day-4 follow-ups on non-replies |
| Week 3 | 75 | ~20 | Tue/Thu/Sat | First demo calls; requalify Pineapple, Seven Stars, Jackalope |
| Week 4 | 50 | ~20 | Tue/Thu/Sat | Hit 200 total; close first pilot; draft case study |

## KPIs

- Emails + DMs sent (target: 200)
- Replies received
- Demo calls booked
- Free pilots running
- Paid clients at £55/month

## Daily execution (5-a-day habit, 60-90 mins)

1. Find the target (10 mins): 5 pubs from `east_london_pubs.csv` with 3.5-4.2 stars and recent unanswered 1-2 star reviews.
2. Generate the value (20 mins): run the worst unanswered review through Claude to draft a pub-voice reply.
3. Send the hook (10 mins): Trojan Horse email/DM with the drafted reply.
4. Follow up (10 mins): day-4 nudge for non-replies.

## Channel rules

- Cold email: max 5 per day, manual sends only
- LinkedIn: Tue/Thu/Sat at 1 PM IST, max 300 words, first line under 8 words, never start with "I", 3-5 hashtags (#AIAutomation #Hospitality #n8n #UKBusiness), end with question OR CTA, no em dashes
- Instagram DM: use the approved script verbatim from [[Brand Messaging — Zero Clicks]]
- Trade press: one pitch to editorial@morningadvertiser.co.uk

## Logging

Log every send in [[Customers/Pipeline]]: date, channel, target, outcome. Evidence before claims.
