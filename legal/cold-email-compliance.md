## Is Cold Email Legal in the UK?

**Yes — B2B cold email is legal in the UK without prior consent**, under PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003).

The rules differ for B2B vs B2C:

| | B2B (pub owners, managers) | B2C (consumers) |
|--|--------------------------|-----------------|
| **Prior consent needed?** | No | Yes (opt-in required) |
| **Legitimate interest applies?** | Yes | No |
| **Must include opt-out?** | Yes | Yes |
| **Must identify sender?** | Yes | Yes |

**Zero Clicks emails pub owners and managers = B2B = legal without opt-in consent.**

- "Quick thing about your Google reviews" = fine

✅ Your current subjects are compliant.

### 3. Clear opt-out mechanism
Every email must give the recipient a way to stop receiving emails from you.

**Minimum acceptable:**
> "Not relevant? Reply with 'remove' and I won't contact you again."

**Better:**
> "If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply to this email and I'll remove you from my list immediately."

⚠️ **Add this to every cold email template.** It's required.

### 4. Honour opt-outs promptly
If someone replies asking to be removed:
- Remove them within 28 days (best practice: same day)
- Never contact them again
- Log the opt-out in your tracker

## ICO Registration — Do You Need It?

**Probably yes.** If you process personal data (names, email addresses) for commercial purposes, you must register with the ICO.

- **Cost:** £40/year (Tier 1 — micro organisations)
- **Register at:** [ico.org.uk/registration](https://ico.org.uk/registration)
- **Time:** 10 minutes online

**Do this before you scale beyond 100 emails.** Low risk of enforcement at small scale, but it's £40 and gives you a registration number to put in your privacy policy.

## GDPR Legitimate Interest Basis

When you collect and store prospect email addresses for outreach, you rely on **Legitimate Interest** as your lawful basis. To use this:

**Three-part test (keep this in mind):**
1. **Purpose test:** Is there a legitimate purpose? ✅ Yes — commercial outreach for a relevant service
2. **Necessity test:** Is email necessary for this purpose? ✅ Yes — it's the standard B2B contact channel
3. **Balancing test:** Does your interest override the individual's rights? ✅ Yes — they're business owners, email is their normal channel, you're offering a relevant service

**Document this:** Keep a simple note that says you've assessed legitimate interest. If the ICO ever asks, you can show you thought about it.
