### 1. WHAT WE WILL DO

Zero Clicks will:

- Connect to your Google Business Profile via secure OAuth (Google's standard connection method)
- Monitor your Google reviews and detect new ones within minutes of posting
- Generate a draft reply to each review using AI, written in your business's tone and voice
- Present drafts in your secure Zero Clicks dashboard for your review and approval
- Post approved replies to Google on your behalf
- Flag negative reviews (1–2 stars) for your immediate attention
- Provide a dashboard showing all reviews, drafts, sent replies, and basic metrics
